ONE PLATFORM · FOUR CAPABILITIES

Turn scattered evidence
into clear next steps.

ResQTrace AI is designed to help responders see what is known, what is uncertain, and where more investigation may be valuable.

01 / ANALYZE

AI Image Analysis

Review aerial imagery for visible people, damaged structures, and relevant objects using a suitable computer-vision model.

Evidence from imagery ↗
02 / MAP

Disaster Memory Map

Keep a location-based record of observations, search status, timestamps, and evidence quality.

A map that remembers ↗
03 / PRIORITIZE

Unknown-Area Detection

Highlight areas that are unsearched, obscured, or supported by outdated evidence instead of assuming they are safe.

Find the information gaps ↗
04 / RESPOND

Adaptive Rescue Planning

Recommend investigation priorities and routes around known hazards as new information becomes available.

Support better decisions ↗
THE CORE INNOVATION

What don't
we know?

A location with no detected survivor isn't automatically safe. An unclear image isn't a completed search. ResQTrace AI keeps these distinctions visible so teams can make better-informed decisions.
